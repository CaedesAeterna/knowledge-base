Most important transhumanists (top 4)

- Nick Bostrom — philosopher; longtermism, existential risk, human enhancement (key work: Superintelligence). [nickbostrom.com](https://nickbostrom.com/papers/a-history-of-transhumanist-thought/)
- Ray Kurzweil — futurist; technological singularity and AI predictions (key work: The Singularity Is Near). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transhumanism)
- Max More — philosopher/organizer; coined modern transhumanism and Extropianism (key texts: Extropian Principles, foundational essays).
- Anders Sandberg — researcher in neuroethics, human enhancement, morphological freedom (important papers and policy work). [nickbostrom.com](https://nickbostrom.com/papers/a-history-of-transhumanist-thought/)

Most important posthumanists (top 4)

- N. Katherine Hayles — literary/cultural theorist; theory of informational/posthuman subjectivity (key work: How We Became Posthuman). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transhumanism)
- Donna Haraway — feminist technoscience theorist; cyborg subjectivity and critique of humanism (key essay: “A Cyborg Manifesto”). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transhumanism)
- Rosi Braidotti — philosopher of posthuman subjectivity and critical posthumanism (key work: The Posthuman). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transhumanism)
- Francesca Ferrando — contemporary philosopher of philosophical posthumanism and new materialisms (key writings on posthumanism theory). [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Transhumanism)
